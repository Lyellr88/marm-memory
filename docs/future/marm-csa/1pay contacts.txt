How to Use This List
Start with 10–15 direct messages: personalize by referencing issues common to their department (“I saw your team deals with escalations around customer promises and callback tracking…”).

Follow with LinkedIn comments/engagement and, if possible, target email based on their linked information.

Prioritize Operations, Disputes, and Engineering for real pain; Executive Ops, Product, and Corporate Development for sponsorship/escalation.

Focus on those likely to “get chewed out in meetings” over call-backs, broken promises, QA misses, or rising support tickets.
